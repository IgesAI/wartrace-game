# ART BRIEF 01 — WESTERN FRONT EXPEDITION (ORIGINAL ART)

## Desired feel
A breathtaking widescreen, subtly animated atmospheric historical landscape for a daily knowledge game. Fine bespoke pixel-art-inspired brushwork, layered silhouettes, earthy material texture, horizon depth and premium editorial UI. Emotional reference: a museum diorama coming alive; curious, solemn and cinematic. NOT a combat action game, not a copy of any existing game interface.

## Palette
Ink green #111715; charcoal #1B241F; parchment #D6CAB0; brass #C4A46D; fog olive #75806F; muted terracotta orange #C57B4B; highlight paper #EFE9DA.

## Scene illustration
A safe fictionalized European WWI-era trench environment with a foreground traversing expedition silhouette, diffuse autumn sky, distant rooflines, earthworks and wind-blown grasses. Avoid specific geographically identifiable battlefield claims. Five to six horizontally seamless parallax layers deliver depth and camera motion, from slow atmospheric sky to fast near-ground textures. Keep center composition dark enough for HUD contrast but not muddy. Optional little historical curiosity vignettes unlock only after question/source reveal.

## Animation direction
Very slow cloud/fog, cloth and grass movement; camera slide proportional to meters; parallax factors 0.08/0.2/0.4/0.75/1.0, configurable. Subtle footstep loop for explorer. Particle count capped on mobile. Screen shake, explosions and gunfire not required. Reduced-motion path crossfades static scenic checkpoints. Scene should be compelling without audio.

## Exact export request
- 1920x1080 master scene/reference image with original layered scene build.
- Separate looping/tiling PNG/WebP sprite layers: sky, distant skyline, near horizon, midground trench structures, traversable ground, foreground debris/grass; compositing notes.
- Protagonist silhouette 8–12 frame walk cycle and idle frame, nonviolent.
- Source license/creator record for every sprite and sound.
- 1200x1200 square result composition and 1080x1920 replay composition, using same art identity.
- Mobile portrait crop strategy and safe text areas.

## Quality rejection conditions
No default icons as landscape; no obvious unlicensed photos or gameplay screenshots; no illegible silhouettes, clipped tiles, accidental modern elements, oversaturated generic stock war imagery, text burned into art, giant visual seam, excessive gore or propagandistic iconography.

## One-paragraph prompt for an image specialist (optional)
'Create original layered 2.5D pixel-art-inspired scenery for a sophisticated history trivia expedition titled WARTRACE: solemn WWI-era European muddy trench outskirts at dawn, distant ruined village silhouettes and clouds, muted ink/olive/parchment/brass palette, subtle atmospheric fog, meticulously crafted hand-placed terrain detail, cinematic side-scrolling parallax layout, deliberately open central UI-safe zone, no text, no logos, no soldiers fighting, no gore; supply independent seamless depth layers and legal attribution metadata rather than a flattened screenshot alone.'
