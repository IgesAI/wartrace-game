# 16 — Monetization and Unit-Economics Strategy

## Sequence
**Beta:** everything playable for free; no paywall, subscriptions or advertising in the timed loop. Validate repeat sessions, share link conversion, and operational cost per completed expedition first.

**After retention evidence:** test optional paid single-era campaign packs ($4.99–$7.99 hypothesized), collectible skins and ad-removal, but keep Daily Operation and source evidence free. Never sell leaderboard advantage or scoring boosts. Explain one-time purchase terms plainly.

**Longer-term:** themed museum partnerships, education licenses and merch if users request them; partnership content must disclose sponsorship and retain editorial independence.

## Model variables to monitor
- Estimated cost per 1,000 completed games: database requests, hosting, media CDN and encode jobs, analytics.
- Content creation cost per 7 reviewed prompts, review/appeal overhead, art maintenance and support tickets.
- Retention by acquisition channel; paid conversion if paid features launch; refunds/chargebacks/merchant fees.
- Ads can damage share and play retention; avoid autoplay audio, intrusive overlays and disruptions during timers.

## Revenue caveats
No credible basis exists here for forecasting traffic or revenue. The competitor's published traffic estimates cannot be treated as paying-customer or profit figures. Present business models as hypotheses and test willingness to pay responsibly.
