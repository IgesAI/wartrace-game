# 07 — Viral Loop, Social Product and Launch Experiment Plan

## Growth strategy
Do not promise virality. Engineer a product with a concise hook, visual proof, a low-friction challenge, and measurable friend conversion. The daily score is public-facing social currency, but no paid rank and no punitive mechanics.

## Signature hook
'Name a WWII fighter aircraft. The rarer your valid answer, the farther you travel across a cinematic battlefield.' Each round produces a visual reward; the seven-round route forms a compelling 10–15-second recap. Promise is understandable even if viewer hasn't played.

## Five share surfaces
1. **1:1 score card:** illustrated horizontal/vertical route, total distance and player-selected name (optional), 7 ticks, 'Beat my expedition' QR/deep link, without raw answers/spoilers.
2. **9:16 replay:** branded 10–15 seconds of generated original parallax traversal, tier flashes and meter count, final distance + CTA; supports muted playback and readable subtitles.
3. **Challenge link:** `/challenge/YYYY-MM-DD?from=...` resolves to that date's challenge if still playable; on expired dates show archive/next daily plus original result context; never expose answers in OG previews.
4. **Discord participation:** shareable text-only result and optional server daily leaderboard in P1 after abuse and identity controls; no mass unsolicited bot messages.
5. **Streamer mode:** huge prompt, hidden player entry/score until reveal, viewer predictions via creator-native tools later; avoid custom Twitch integration in MVP.

## Viral flywheel
Play -> discover unusual answer -> visual payoff -> produce copyable replay/card -> friend views spoiler-free challenge -> friend plays same game -> compares -> daily return. Instrument each handoff. Share features must work even if WebM/MP4 capture is unsupported (card fallback). Challenge URLs must not be guessable private user paths.

## Creator campaign
- Target educational history creators, aviation enthusiasts, museum accounts, WWII researchers, engineering channels, niche trivia streamers and thoughtful games journalists.
- Avoid manufactured screenshots or fake 'millions of users' claims. Send a simple playable URL and 10s product reel with a distinct ask: 'See if you can beat this obscure answer.'
- Product launch deliverables: teaser video, 1-page press kit, cropped gameplay gif, complete landing page, public privacy/source policy, consent-based email waitlist optional, OG image, Discord community rules and report channel.

## Starter content experiments
| Test | Variant A | Variant B | Primary measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| First fold | Daily prompt teaser | Full scenic progression teaser | Play-start conversion |
| Share framing | Total meters | Rarest-answer tier | Shared-link started game |
| Onboarding | Skip intro | 3-step intro | Round 1 success + overall completion |
| Reveal pacing | 0.8s camera | 1.6s cinematic camera | Drop-off after round |
| Creator hook | 'Rarest valid aircraft' | 'How far can you go?' | External video-to-start conversion |

Do not randomize a shared daily score, question order or published rarity across A/B cohorts; experiment only on presentation/retention features. Avoid violating privacy or experimental research ethics.

## Day-0 to Day-30 plan
**Prelaunch:** private QA with 20–50 diverse testers, source disputes captured, iOS/Android screenshots, low-end benchmark; fix trust problems first. Create short accurate teaser.
**Launch week:** 5–10 creators offered playable preview (no undisclosed payments), daily posts with one interesting answer highlight after challenge window closes, observe funnel daily.
**Week 2:** refine first 10 seconds and share card based on drop-off; publish evidence/acceptance improvements; measure actual referral performance.
**Weeks 3–4:** one additional environment only if D1/D7 retention and share conversion trend upward; content partnership outreach with archives/museums after obtaining permission.

## Guardrails
- Avoid spoilers in social preview, scraped contact lists, forced invites, false countdown pressure, fake leaderboard bots, fabricated creators or invented virality.
- Do not incentivize graphic war themes or extreme ideologies to increase clicks.
- Distinguish game world travel from historical movement. Image/video must be clearly 'WARTRACE gameplay', not a real war photograph.
- No off-platform campaign sent without approval. User must authorize paid ads and deployment.

## Decision metrics
North-star proxy: completed daily games leading to a day-1 return or verified referral start. Track start-to-completion, share-copy, recipient click, clicked-to-start, clicked-to-complete, D1/D7 cohort retention, average first-session duration, appeal ratio, and render failures. Targets are hypotheses only. A product is not 'viral' merely because a reel looks polished.
