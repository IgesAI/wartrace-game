# 13 — Competitive Reference: Krillion and Differentiation
**Research snapshot:** 2026-11-22 | **Scope:** public research, no privileged Krillion access.

## What Krillion appears to demonstrate
Krillion offers a short daily open-answer rarity game with an ocean-descent visual metaphor, community comparison and paid additional content. The prior research in this conversation examined official FAQ/privacy statements and public archives. Its precise data-provider list, ingestion schedule, internal rarity formula, revenue and database design are **not confirmed**. Do not copy any game source, data dumps, artistic assets, branding or user interface.

## Reusable product insights, not copied proprietary implementation
1. An easy-to-understand metaphor makes rarity visible and watchable.
2. Equal daily questions make comparisons intuitive and time-bounded.
3. Open answers create surprising outcomes and discussions.
4. Timed sessions are short enough for repeat play and content creation.
5. Game-sharing mechanics matter as much as the answer database.
6. Incomplete broad answer sets erode trust; cited eligibility and appeals are differentiation opportunities.

## WARTRACE's four differentiators
- **Evidence first:** answer source and editorial eligibility are transparent.
- **History as expedition:** original side-scrolling world, not ocean-depth reskin.
- **Stable daily scoring:** no live popularity-driven retrospective revisions.
- **Built-for-sharing replay:** automatically legible 1:1 and 9:16 recap, with spoiler-safe friend route.

## Positioning language
'An atmospheric daily history expedition where obscure correct answers take you farther.' Avoid saying 'Krillion but war' in public brand copy. Strong category: **historical knowledge adventure**, not war-simulation game.

## Competitive unknowns requiring caution
- Krillion does not publish a verifiable full upstream data-source inventory.
- Traffic estimates from third parties are estimates, not audited users.
- Pricing and purchases can change; payment funnel is not accessible without separate checkout verification.
- An external audit cannot establish internal security controls or exact app endpoints without approved technical inspection.
- No evidence proves WARTRACE will exceed competitor traction; launch hypotheses must be tested.
