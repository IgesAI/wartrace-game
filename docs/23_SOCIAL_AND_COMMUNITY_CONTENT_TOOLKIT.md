## Seven repeatable organic formats
| Format | Length | Structure | Why someone shares | Production dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SECOND_ANSWER` | 8s video | common answer → obscurity reveal → travel | 'I can beat that' | valid rarity pair, real run |
| `RAREST_THIS_WEEK` | 1:1 card | one unusual answer, abbreviated why | unexpected discovery | historian-approved claim |
| `ONE_MORE_NAME` | short X post | 'Most know X; name another in 25s' | competitive replies | eligible bounded question |
| `MAP_REVEAL` | 12s vertical | advance from 0 to a landmark | satisfy visual progression | final scene art |
| `ARCHIVE_RECEIPT` | 4-slide carousel | fascinating item → citation → how it relates | credibility / saves | cleared archive media |
| `CREATOR_CHALLENGE` | 15s split-screen | same prompt, two people | share with a friend | actual permission/recordings |
| `EDITORIAL_TRANSPARENCY` | thread/post | question conflict and fair fix | builds trust | approved correction log |

## Vertical clip scripting template (11 seconds)
- **0.0–1.1s HOOK:** text `Name another WWII fighter.` over gameplay, not archival combat footage.
- **1.1–2.9s TENSION:** `Someone typed [verified obscure answer]` + short typed animation (only from real test run).
- **2.9–6.7s PAYOFF:** distant landmark revealed, distance climbs, camera advances.
- **6.7–9.0s PROOF:** source button briefly visible + rarity label.
- **9.0–11.0s CTA:** `Your turn. Today's operation.` with legible URL/overlay. Include captions and alt-text in post.

## Social cards: metadata spec
**OG image:** 1200×630 for link unfurls (separate from 1:1 share card). Spoiler-safe: today's operation art, “7 questions / 1 shared challenge”, generic progress marker; no hidden answers. **1:1 card:** 1200×1200 shows player-approved score and world distance, rarest answer only if opt-in. **Vertical:** 1080×1920, safe center 1080×1420 so text not clipped by platform UI. Format/performance must be tested in actual apps; these are production targets, not a guarantee of placement.

## Social moderation and operations
- Never debate genuine historical harm as 'content'. Moderate hate speech, victim mockery, dehumanizing language and fabricated atrocity claims quickly and consistently.
- Factual answer complaints: link to correction/appeal workflow and, when resolved, cite the record.
- Community notes/pins: mark spoilers and do not leak a still-active daily accepted-answer list.
- Disclose paid creators/sponsorships in the appropriate channel and follow platform rules.
- Avoid automated unsolicited outreach, scraped DMs, fake social proof, bought followers and misleading history footage.

## Accessibility and content ethics
Every video needs captions and an informative title. Avoid strobing and real traumatic footage in growth promos. For archival imagery state when an image is a historical photograph; for gameplay explicitly label it as original game art. Provide a text-based share route, not only video.

## Mini press kit checklist
1. One-sentence pitch; 2. 100-word description; 3. logo and marks (original); 4. screenshots from actual playable build; 5. trailer/clips; 6. founding story (verified personal details only with approval); 7. media licensing/attributions; 8. contact URL; 9. exact launch status; 10. FAQ with sources and criticism/corrections policy.

## Launch narrative positioning
**Lead with the experience:** 'A history game where the weirdest correct answer moves you through the world.' **Prove differentiation:** real verified sources, beautiful journey, no account needed. **Then let communities find a reason to argue about historical minutiae** within civil, evidence-based discussion. Do not over-promise that it will go viral.
